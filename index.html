Doxli Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Doxli is an offline PDF reader and local document organiser developed by NLR Group of Companies. This Privacy Policy explains how the Doxli Android application accesses and handles information.

Information We Collect

Doxli does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

PDF Files and Device Access

Doxli uses Android's system file picker to allow users to select PDF documents and folders. The app accesses only files and folders selected by the user.

PDF files are processed locally on the user's device. They are not uploaded to Doxli, the developer or any external server.

Local App Data

Features such as recent documents and folder organisation may store document references, preferences and organisational information locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide Doxli's functionality and is not transmitted outside the device.

Data Sharing

Doxli does not share user data with third parties. Google Play may process information independently when users download or update the app, according to Google's own privacy practices.

Data Security

Doxli limits file access through Android's system permissions and Storage Access Framework. User documents remain under the user's control on their device.

Data Retention and Deletion

Doxli does not retain user data on developer-controlled servers. Users can remove locally stored app information by clearing Doxli's app data or uninstalling the application.

Original PDF documents remain in locations controlled by the user unless the user chooses to delete them.

Children's Privacy

Doxli is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Doxli's features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated whenever a new version is published.
